# Ravinder & Kavita Wedding Invitation

An interactive, royal Indian wedding invitation webpage featuring:
- Realistic 3D Royal Burgundy Envelope with wax seal and authentic opening animation
- Sacred rotating religious greetings (Shree Ganesh, Ek Onkar, Om Namah Shivaya, Shubh Vivah)
- Background wedding instrumental music with toggle control
- Interactive "Scratch to reveal the date" cards with countdown timer
- "A Legacy of Love" coverflow photo carousel with 3-second autoplay
- Sacred ceremonies & celebrations section with interactive Google Maps integration
- Designed & Developed by [Inhove](https://inhove.com/)
